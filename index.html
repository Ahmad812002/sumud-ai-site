Tell it about yourself once. Use it on every application.

Generic chatbots forget you at the end of each session. Sumud AI stores your documents and retrieves the relevant parts each time, so the answers reflect your real experience.

  1. Add your documents

    Upload your CV, project write-ups and notes as PDF, Word, Markdown or text. Sumud AI splits them into searchable pieces and stores them in your profile.

  2. Paste a job posting

    You get a fit score, the skills you match and the gaps, based on what your documents actually say.

  3. Review the draft

    Get a cover letter written from your own experience, in the language of the posting. Edit it, then send it yourself.

Built for people starting their careers

Sumud (صمود) means steadfastness. A first job search can take months and hundreds of applications. We are building for students and recent graduates, including international students applying in Germany, and for the universities that support them.
